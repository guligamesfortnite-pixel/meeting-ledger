Privacy Policy

Last updated: this is a starting template, not reviewed by a lawyer - see the note at the bottom.

1. What we collect

Your name and email (to create your account), the meeting notes, transcripts, and documents you choose to store, and basic technical information needed to keep the service secure (like login attempts, for protecting against abuse).

2. How it's used

To run the service - generating summaries, action items, and reminders - and for nothing beyond that. Your data isn't sold, and isn't shared with other users; every account's meetings stay private to that account.

3. Third parties involved

Meeting summaries may be generated using Anthropic's Claude API, when configured. Password reset emails are sent via a standard email provider. No other third party receives your data.

4. How long data is kept

For as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and everything in it at any time.

5. Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing (never in plain text), and every login session is protected against common attacks (CSRF, brute-force attempts).

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data at any time from your account settings, or by asking directly.

This is a plain-language starting template, not legal advice, and hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. Privacy law varies significantly by country and region (e.g. GDPR in Europe) - before relying on this with real paying customers, especially internationally, it's worth having an actual lawyer review and adapt it.
