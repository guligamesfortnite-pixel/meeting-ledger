One round, three phases

How a round actually plays

Every round repeats the same shape. The order matters, so these are numbered — you cannot vote before a team is proposed, and you cannot quest before a vote passes.

PHASE 01

The Leader proposes

The Leader hands out Team tokens — the number is set by the quest track. The Leader may put themselves on the team, but doesn't have to. One token per player.

PHASE 02

Everyone votes

All players, Leader included, choose Approve or Reject in secret, then reveal together. A majority approves; a tie is a rejection. Reject and leadership passes clockwise.

5 rejections in one round → Evil wins outright

PHASE 03

The team quests

Each team member secretly plays one Quest card. The Leader shuffles them before revealing. A single Fail sinks the quest — except the 4th quest with 7+ players, which needs two.

Let this page run it

One phone passed around the table. It deals every role in secret, narrates the night out loud, collects the votes and the quest cards, and holds the blade until the end.

Narration

    Game end

    Four ways it ends

    The game stops the instant a third quest resolves either way — and then, if Good got there, one more thing happens.

    Good wins

    Three quests succeed

    …and the Assassin then fails to name Merlin.

    Evil wins

    Three quests fail

    The game ends immediately. No assassination needed.

    Evil wins

    Five teams rejected

    Five consecutive failed votes in a single round hands the game to Mordred.

    Evil wins

    Merlin is named

    Three successes on the board, and the blade still finds him.

    Evil's last chance

    If Good completes three quests, play does not stop. Without revealing a single Character card, the Evil players confer openly and the holder of the Assassin card names one Good player as Merlin. Name him and Evil takes the game after all. This is why Merlin spends five rounds steering the table while pretending to guess.

    Optional rules

    Once the table knows the game

    Lady of the Lake

    Open information about someone's loyalty — used three times, and it moves.

    • At setup, the token goes to the player on the Leader's right.
    • Immediately after the 2nd, 3rd and 4th quests resolve, its holder examines one player.
    • The examined player passes the Loyalty card matching their true side — passing the wrong one loses the game.
    • The token then moves to the player just examined. Nobody can be examined twice, and a former holder can't be targeted.
    • The holder may talk about what they saw, but must not reveal the card. Best at 7+; it favours Good.

    Targeting

    Quests attempted in any order the Leader likes — planning replaces routine.

    • In the Team phase the Leader picks both the team and which quest they're attempting.
    • The team size must match the quest chosen; mark it with the Round marker before the vote.
    • A quest attempted once cannot be attempted again.
    • The 5th quest is locked until two other quests have succeeded.
    • At 7+ players the 4th quest still needs two Fails.

    Plot & Loyalty cards

    For tables mixing in Plot cards from The Resistance.

    • Whenever a Plot card would reveal your Character card, hand over the matching Loyalty card instead.
    • Merlin's identity stays secret even when his loyalty is exposed.
    • Passing a Loyalty card that doesn't match your Character card loses the game.
